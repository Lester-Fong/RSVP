# ALB-00 — Our Wedding Memories: product and architecture plan

Date: 2026-10-25. **Documentation only; no album implementation or activation.** D-051 records the business requirements; D-052 approves ALB-D01's explicit initial reveal and subsequent Update revealed album contract. D-053 records the owner-preferred deployment candidate for evaluation, not an approved or verified production configuration. Other proposed architecture/schema/access decisions remain open. Source inspection and provider documentation are not deployment verification.

## 1. Approved product direction

The primary business is a premium **wedding website service** with secure personal RSVP, DIY customization and optional paid done-for-you setup/customization. It is not a general wedding-management application. Existing guest/RSVP capabilities support that service. Advanced drag-and-drop Seating is outside MVP priorities and deferred indefinitely unless actual customer demand justifies reopening it. Preserve the historical Seating proposals and any existing work; do not replace that deferral with a new basic planner commitment. 5D withdrawal, internal administration, security/deployment and pilot safeguards remain independent requirements.

**Our Wedding Memories** is a separate, higher-value **paid add-on**, excluded from the lowest website package. It is an emotional, romantic, elegant and nostalgic post-wedding experience, not another CMS show/hide section. Its collection, moderation and reveal state remain independent of the invitation's existing **10-image gallery** and immutable website revisions.

## 2. Independent state and access contract — ALB-D01 approved; other mechanics proposed

Keep collection, moderation, reveal and entitlement separate. Hiding an album is not deletion, rejecting a photo is not deletion, and closing collection need not hide an already revealed album. Whether collection may open before the wedding and how its closing window works are commercial decisions, not inferred from the post-wedding reveal requirement.

| State/action                 | Recommended meaning; subject to approval                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Upload accepted              | Processing succeeds and private metadata/bytes commit; status Pending. A browser progress bar reaching 100% is not completion. Failed/unfinished files remain unavailable.                                                                                 |
| Approve / reject             | Owner version-checked moderation; approval permits later release, rejection/removal denies visitor delivery immediately. Retain underlying media until a separately approved deletion policy applies.                                                      |
| Feature / unfeature          | Curate approved favorites for Our Highlights; deterministic order with ID tie-breakers. Never makes a pending/rejected photo visible. Caption/highlight and other presentation edits stay private until explicit Update revealed album (approved ALB-D01). |
| Reveal                       | Explicit confirmation after the wedding, rechecking entitlement, owner authority and expected album version under the wedding lock. Release the selected approved set; do not publish CMS drafts.                                                          |
| Hide / unreveal              | Immediately deny album page, thumbnails, full images and downloads; retain photos/moderation/release membership privately. Collection permission stays a separate switch.                                                                                  |
| Disable / expire entitlement | Deny administration, collection and visitor delivery; retain data. Recovery/export access, renewal, grace periods and eventual deletion need separate terms and staff authority.                                                                           |
| Re-reveal                    | Recommend reviewing the retained released set and explicitly including any newly approved additions. No automatic release of everything approved while hidden.                                                                                             |

Recommended visitor predicate: **feature available + active wedding entitlement + valid viewing grant + album revealed + approved and explicitly released photo/presentation + same-wedding, album-purpose media + completed processing + no removal/revocation**. Apply it to every page/list/thumbnail/image/HEAD/download request, not just navigation. Owners need authenticated, tenant-scoped private review routes that do not grant visitor authority. No RSVP guest names, responses, contacts or bearer credentials enter album props or CMS content.

### ALB-D01: approvals after reveal — owner-approved 2026-10-25

**Approved: explicit “Update revealed album”.** Initial reveal remains an explicit couple action. After reveal, newly approved photos and presentation edits are staged privately until the couple explicitly publishes an update. Visitors continue seeing the last released presentation while those changes are staged. Removal/rejection of previously released content takes effect immediately, without waiting for an update. Approval alone never reveals an album or adds a photo to an already revealed album.

Caption, highlight and ordering/presentation edits must not leak through reads of mutable moderation fields. The implementation must keep staged presentation separate from released presentation while applying current rejection/removal checks to all delivery paths. Private owner preview shows staged changes; it does not grant visitor access. Previously discussed immediate appearance on approval was not selected.

This approves behavior, not a schema or implementation batch. A small released set plus released presentation values/version is a proposal; membership alone cannot protect staged caption/highlight edits. Reuse focused relational actions and wedding-first locks, without cloning the immutable invitation publishing system. Re-reveal/window/date mechanics remain ALB-D04; they must not bypass explicit release or make a hidden album public accidentally. ALB-D02–07 still need their scoped approvals before implementation.

## 4. Security, privacy and transaction gates

1. **Access:** sharing a link gives its recipient that capability; it does not verify identity or wedding attendance. Recommend exchanging each purpose-specific token for a short-lived scoped HttpOnly/SameSite cookie, then redirecting to a clean URL. Revalidate grant version/expiry, entitlement and relevant album/photo state on every operation. No guest account requirement; no reuse of personal RSVP sessions or credentials. Define secure exchange, CSRF and HTTPS behavior before implementation.
2. **Viewing distribution:** entitlement and reveal are necessary but insufficient for access. Recommend showing Our Memories only to an already authorized viewer on a revealed/entitled wedding; owners share a separate viewing link. Alternatively a generic nav entry may lead to a protected access screen, never embed a viewing bearer link into generic HTML. Approve this choice; a hidden nav link is not protection. QR upload recipients must not automatically gain gallery access.
3. **Private delivery:** no public bucket/static original paths, unscoped temporary URLs or source filenames. Recheck authorization for each rendition and download; safe Content-Type/Disposition, no-sniff, private/no-store, no-referrer/noindex and same-origin delivery. Tokens/IDs/storage paths must not leak through social metadata, analytics or error reporting. If future signed storage URLs/cache/CDN delivery are proposed, disclose their revocation/cache expiry window; already downloaded copies cannot be recalled.
4. **Upload validation:** allow-list detected MIME/content and expected extension, successful bounded decode, dimensions/pixels/memory/time, orientation and re-encoding/metadata stripping; reject active formats, malformed files and decompression bombs. Generate paths and download names server-side. Never serve quarantined originals or failed processing output. Scan/processing requirements must match the chosen runtime and threat model, not an unverified “safe upload” claim.
5. **Abuse and capacity:** layer per-grant, wedding/account and IP rate controls, request/file/aggregate byte budgets and storage reservations; shared venue/mobile networks require fairness. Quotas must include pending/rejected/retained photos, originals, derivatives and unfinished reservations. UI caps are not enforcement. Handle concurrent final-capacity uploads and retries without double charging or orphaning assets.
6. **Transactions:** preserve wedding-first lock order, then entitlement/album/grant/photo/media rows in deterministic order. Revalidate authority/state inside the transaction, use expected versions for moderation/reveal/bulk actions and reject stale selections atomically. Decode expensive files outside long wedding locks, reserving bounded capacity first and rechecking before commit. Grants disabled during processing must prevent completion becoming visible. File writes are not SQL-atomic; retain files on unknown commit outcomes and reconcile conservatively (RF-030).
7. **Mobile reliability:** one file receipt per completed upload, bounded client concurrency, per-file retry, cancellation and reload guidance. Use scoped idempotency for lost acknowledgments; successful files should not be resent with the failed remainder. Never infer success from local previews or expose other uploaders' pending IDs/counts/photos. Durable processing/queues only if justified by measured workloads and approved hosting; no new package by assumption.
8. **Moderation/consent:** plain escaped text, short validated lengths to approve, no arbitrary HTML or external-image URLs. Explain couple-controlled guest-photo publication, photographer/uploader rights, minors/bystanders, caption/name visibility, download implications and removal/reporting. Owner approval is not legal consent from every depicted person; establish a practical removal contact and incident procedure before real collection.
9. **Retention/recovery:** define collection closure, link expiry, entitlement suspension, grace/recovery/export windows, deletion notice and backup expiry separately. Hide, reject, unpublish and entitlement expiry do not silently destroy files. Purging requires audited authorization and all retained-reference checks. No deletion-on-error or deletion when DB outcome is unknown; restored backups must not reactivate revoked access.

The album's behavior when the invitation is unpublished, rolled back, changes template/slug or the wedding date is edited remains **ALB-D04**, not implied by independent content. Recommend denying visitor album delivery while the invitation is unpublished, retaining collection/moderation data, and using the current published wedding's canonical date/timezone for post-wedding reveal checks. Exact “after wedding” cutoff and missing-time behavior need approval; never invent midnight or silently alter countdown/publication facts.

## 5. Formats, quality and cost planning

Current source accepts JPEG/PNG/WebP only: **5 MiB source, 12 MP, 6,000px dimension, static WebP output up to 1,600px, originals discarded**. Those accepted invitation limits stay intact. Changing shared constants to 10 MB would neither provide original retention nor meet album download-quality needs.

For the album, plan bounded JPEG/PNG/WebP ingestion with strictly private originals and metadata-stripped display/thumb/download derivatives. Private originals are an approved security requirement; their retention duration, processing representation, derivative resolution/quality, animated input handling and whether owners may export raw originals need explicit contracts. Never expose original EXIF/GPS through visitor downloads. Reuse the asset registry with a minimal private rendition model if justified; avoid forcing arbitrary originals into the current WebP-only storage validator.

**HEIC is not supported by current code.** PHP's documented GD decoder formats do not include HEIC, and format support depends on the build ([PHP manual](https://www.php.net/manual/en/function.imagecreatefromstring.php)). Apple documents HEIF capture and manual JPEG/PNG export; automatic conversion happens only in some sharing paths, so it cannot be promised for a website picker ([Apple support](https://support.apple.com/en-ph/116944)). These sources were checked 2026-10-25; no real-device/production-codec test was run.

Smallest initial proposal: explicitly reject unsupported HEIC with considerate conversion instructions and preserve the user's other successful files; do not promise automatic browser conversion. Before choosing this fallback, assess actual iPhone camera/library uploads, rotation/color/metadata, memory and conversion quality. Native conversion support would require an approved codec/dependency/runtime and security/cost assessment; no install is authorized by ALB-00. HEIC product support is an owner decision, not silently deferred acceptance of broken iPhone uploads.

Estimate originals + preview/thumb/download renditions + quarantined/rejected/failed uploads + backups/replication + temporary exports; separately estimate requests, processing, download egress, monitoring and support. No provider prices, margins or “free/unlimited” storage assumptions are invented. Retention and download frequency can dominate cost even with modest image counts. Approve technical byte/pixel/request ceilings separately from commercial package quotas.
