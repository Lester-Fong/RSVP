## 4. Responsive, motion and accessibility gaps

- **Responsive:** no desktop/tablet invitation frames were found. Proposed QA sizes are 390 × 844, source-width 414 × 736, transition 1024 × 768 and desktop 1440 × 1000, plus tall captures matching `2:70`/`12:9`/`16:95`. These are test proposals, not extracted desktop design dimensions. Obtain approved desktop/tablet compositions or explicitly approved responsive rules before implementation; do not invent a high-fidelity desktop source. Test long Philippine names, wrapping, text zoom, safe areas and short/landscape screens. Decorations may overlap; content must flow without horizontal scrolling.
- **Opening:** `107:155` has a click navigation to `2:70` with `transition: null`; the nested button also contains an empty click action. No flap, seal, bloom animation, duration, music, skip or replay behavior is specified by this source. Preserve the static cover as the candidate baseline; separately approve any opening motion.
- **Gallery:** three image click reactions navigate to `38:115`, `36:99`, `36:92` with top slide-in, ease-out, approximately 300ms. Close controls use Back. Translate into an accessible lightbox only after its detailed source review; reduced motion needs immediate presentation, keyboard close and focus restoration.
- **Readability:** calculated from source solid sRGB tokens using relative luminance: white on `#DEB3AD` ≈1.88:1, white on `#B95C50` ≈4.46:1, terracotta on blush ≈3.58:1, ochre on blush ≈4.49:1. These are token-pair calculations, not browser accessibility results; they show that some small text needs changed colors/size/weight. Review actual rendered backgrounds/font sizes; propose darker text/accent variants with contrast safeguards and record exact approved values. Do not round a ratio below 4.5 up to a normal-text pass.
- **Controls:** enlarge small map/lightbox/navigation targets without relying on tiny icons/text; provide semantic buttons/links, labels, focus, Enter/Space, touch and visible skip. No-JS must expose wedding content and a working anchor. Existing JavaScript-required RSVP submission remains an explicit limit.
